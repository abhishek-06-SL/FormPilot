# FormPilot
A smart browser extension that simplifies online form filling with real-time field explanations in English and Hindi.
ABOUT:
FormPilot is an extension which helps people to fill complex and difficult to understand forms in simple way. It do so by explaining exactly what to do in each field.
It helps in understanding complex words and tell what mistakes not to take without you bothering to google it or ask AI.
SETUP:
Save all these files in one folder in your local space.
Go to chrome and click on extensions, then on manage extensions and enable developer mode.
click on load unpacked and select that folder in which your files were saved.
Now your extension is good to go.
